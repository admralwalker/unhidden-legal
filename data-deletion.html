Data Deletion Instructions

The UnHidden Presents publishing tool does not collect personal data from visitors or followers. It only handles content and access tokens for our own accounts.

To request deletion of any information you believe we hold about you, email YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL_HERE with the subject "Data deletion request" and include the name or handle you used. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.
