Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy applies to the publishing tool used by UnHidden Presents ("we", "us"), a music label run by Admral. The tool is used only by us, to schedule and publish our own posts, videos and clips to our own Facebook Page, Instagram account and Threads account. It is not offered to the public.

What we collect

The tool connects to our own accounts through Meta's APIs. It stores access tokens for those accounts and the content we choose to publish. It does not collect personal information from visitors, followers or other users, and it does not use cookies or trackers.

How we use it

We use the connection only to publish our own approved content and to read basic post performance on our own accounts. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Retention and security

Access tokens are kept in a private, access-controlled secret store. We keep published-content records only as long as we need them and delete tokens when the connection is removed.

Your rights and deletion

If you believe we hold any of your information, or you want it deleted, see our data deletion page or contact us at the address below.
